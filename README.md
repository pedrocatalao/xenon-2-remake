# Xenon 2 Megablast remake

A faithful remake of **Xenon 2 Megablast** (The Bitmap Brothers, 1989) for modern computers, made with Godot, together with an **ENHANCED** edition that brings the game to high resolution screens.


## The game

The complete game, as it was in 1989:

- all five levels with their mid-level and end-of-level guardians;
- the shop with every weapon and upgrade, buying and selling, in the middle and at the end of each level;
- pods, power-ups and tokens: speed, power, rate of fire, dives, energy, side shots, the Z bomb, shields, Super Nashwan Power;
- dive, protection and bitmap shades;
- lives, continues, the high score table, one or two players in turn;
- the end of the game and the loop back to the first level.

The behaviour follows the original game to the frame: the same enemy waves, paths, speeds, collisions and timings.

## PC or Amiga

The title menu chooses, at any time:

- **graphics**: the PC (VGA) version or the Amiga version, with its own level backgrounds, title screen and colours;
- **sound**: the PC speaker or the Amiga sound, with the Megablast music and the Amiga sound effects.

## ORIGINAL 1989 or ENHANCED 2026

The opening screen offers two editions, and **F12** switches between them at any moment, even in the middle of a level.

| | ORIGINAL 1989 | ENHANCED 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Picture | the original 320 × 200 picture, enlarged | every picture drawn sharp at the resolution of your screen |
| Graphics | the original pixels | smooth high resolution graphics, with the ship, its weapons, power-ups and title logos redrawn |
| Amiga music | the in-game arrangement | the full arrangement of the original release, with its scratches and voices |

Both editions play exactly the same: only the look and the sound change.

## Building

Open the folder in **Godot 4.7.2** (standard build) and run it, or export it from *Project > Export* for Windows or Linux. Ready builds are on the Releases page.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrows or keypad | move |
| Space or joystick buttons | fire |
| Enter | dive |
| P | pause |
| Esc | back to the title |
| F12 | ORIGINAL or ENHANCED |
| F10 | quit |
| K, on the title menu | choose your own keys |
